Chand Baori: The Stepwell of 3,500 Steps

Abhaneri, Rajasthan • 9th Century CE

⏱ 4:48 mins

Chand Baori is one of the oldest and deepest stepwells in the world. Built by King Chanda of the Nikumbha Dynasty in the 9th century, its geometric perfection served as a community reservoir and sanctuary against the scorching Thar desert heat.

The Secret of the Rustless Iron Pillar

Qutub Complex, New Delhi • 4th Century CE (Gupta Empire)

✔ You discovered the archaeological truth!

The 6-ton, 7.2-meter tall pillar has resisted monsoon rains and sun for over 1,600 years without rusting. Ancient Indian metallurgists achieved this by forge-welding wrought iron with a high phosphorus content and low sulfur/manganese, forming a protective misawite (crystalline iron hydrogen phosphate) passive layer on the surface!

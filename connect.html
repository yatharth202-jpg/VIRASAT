How "Find the Connection" Works

A digital game celebrating human ingenuity across Indian traditions.

1. Select Your State & Domain: Choose from Uttar Pradesh (UP), Punjab, Rajasthan, Gujarat, Kerala, or Karnataka.

2. Guess the Underlying Link: Hypothesize whether the two cultures share water management, Natya Shastra roots, counted-thread embroidery, or acoustic vaulting.

3. Reveal & Deep-Dive: Uncover the scholarly comparison, explore architectural similarities and dissimilarities, and read full Wikipedia archives.

4. Earn Passport Stamps: Every solved connection adds a verified stamp to your Virasat Passport with its key similarity breakdown.

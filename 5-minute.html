5-MINUTE LESSON

Mastering Bharatanatyam Mudras

Pataka • Tripataka • Ardhapataka

1. Pataka: Extend all four fingers straight together and bend the thumb touching the base of the index finger. Used to denote clouds, forest, or open sky.

2. Tripataka: From Pataka, bend only the ring finger downwards. Used to depict a crown, tree, or lightning bolt.

3. Ardhapataka: From Tripataka, bend both the ring finger and the little finger. Used to signify a knife, bank of a river, or a flag.

# Design Specification: Virasat Elder Wisdom Vault

## Overview
A high-fidelity cultural heritage and elder wisdom preservation platform. The design is a sophisticated, "Antigravity-ready" desktop web dashboard that combines a premium historical aesthetic with modern user interface patterns.

## Visual Identity (Aetheris Design System)
- **Theme**: Dark, scholarly, and atmospheric.
- **Color Palette**:
  - **Primary**: Muted Gold (#C5A059) for icons and accents.
  - **Surface**: Deep Mahogany / Charcoal Brown (#1A1412) for the header.
  - **Background**: Aged Parchment / Warm Cream (#F5E6D3) with a realistic paper fiber texture.
  - **Cards**: Light Parchment (#FFF9F0) with soft thin brown borders.
  - **Accents**: Earthy Terracotta (#8B5E3C) for primary buttons like "Contribute Now".
  - **Special Section**: "How Your Contribution Helps" uses a specific muted green shade (#E8EDDE).
- **Typography**: Libre Caslon Text (Serif) for a classic, authoritative feel. High contrast deep mahogany (#3E2723) for body text.

## Layout Structure
1. **Global Header**: Dark themed with 'VIRASAT' branding, search, notifications, and user profile.
2. **Hero Section**: 
   - Full-width background image with a warm, amber-tinted sepia filter.
   - Centered "ELDER WISDOM VAULT" title and tagline.
   - Impact statistics (Stories, Elders, Languages, Communities) in a single horizontal row.
   - Featured quote card in aged parchment cream on the right.
3. **Main Content (2-Column)**:
   - **Left Column (Main)**:
     - Tab navigation: Featured, Recent Stories, By Region, By Topic, By Language.
     - 2x2 Grid of "Featured Stories" with high-quality thumbnails, category tags (Recipe, Craft, Tradition, Folk Tale), and save icons.
     - "Explore by Topic" horizontal scroll bar with icon-led cards.
     - "Stories in Your Language" tag cloud.
   - **Right Column (Sidebar)**:
     - "Share Your Wisdom" section with Record, Upload, and Nominate actions.
     - "Wisdom Impact" statistics block.
     - "How Your Contribution Helps" (Muted Green Box): Compact list of benefits (Preserves Heritage, Educates Youth, Builds Community).

## Technical Requirements
- **Responsiveness**: Optimized for desktop viewports (1200px - 1440px container).
- **Interactions**: Smooth hover states on story cards and navigation elements.
- **Assets**: Utilizes warm, atmospheric photography and custom traditional lantern/lightlamp imagery.
